Recto PDF · Public beta · Apache-2.0

A PDF editor that runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Open many PDFs in one place. Arrange their pages, mark them up, fill and sign, redact, edit text, recognise scans and compare versions.

Open the app GitHub
  • Your files stay on your device. The engines run inside the tab, and the page may only connect to its own origin.
  • Many files, one workspace. Move pages between documents and edit them in the same session, with undo.
  • Tested on three engines. Every change passes Chromium, Firefox and WebKit before it lands.
