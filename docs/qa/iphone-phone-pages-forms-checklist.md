# iPhone hand check: forms, pages and pinch to overview

The owner's hand check for the phone work of 2026-10-14 (decisions MOB-1 to MOB-5). Safari on an
iPhone, the sample document (`?sample`) and any outside PDF with form fields and several pages.

## Forms (MOB-1)

1. Page 5 of the sample (the contributor agreement): tap the "I have read this agreement" box
   while reading. It ticks; an Undo toast shows
   and takes the tick back.
2. Tap the name field. The keyboard rises in the same tap, the page zooms so the text is easy to
   read, and the page itself does not jump-zoom.
3. Type a name (with ğ, ş, İ), then › on the bar above the keyboard: the date field takes the
   keyboard without it dropping. Done: the zoom returns.
4. With the Pen armed, a tap on a field draws, it does not fill.
5. Save a copy and open it elsewhere (Files, Preview): the values are there.

## Pinch to see every page (MOB-2)

6. On a page at normal size, pinch two fingers together: the page shrinks into its tile and the
   grid grows around it; past a point "Release to see all pages" shows. Let go: the grid settles.
7. Pinch inside the grid: 2 · 3 · 4 columns. Spread on a tile, or tap it: you are back on that page.
8. A pinch that starts on the top bar or the capsule does not zoom the whole Safari page.
9. The Pages button opens the same grid with the same motion.

## Pages on the phone (MOB-3)

10. In the grid, press and hold a page: it is selected; taps add more. Rotate, Delete, then Undo.
11. Press, hold and move a page to another place; the grid scrolls near its edges.
12. ＋ Add → Pages from a file: pick a PDF from Files; its pages come after the page you read.
13. Select pages → ⋯ → Extract as file: the share sheet offers one PDF; the open document is unchanged.
14. ⋯ → Split here: the share sheet offers the parts.

Report a failing step with its number, the document and a screen recording.
