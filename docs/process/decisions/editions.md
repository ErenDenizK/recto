| EDN-17 | **Editing text on phones (WP5).** Edit text is live (no longer "Soon"): with it armed, a tap on a detected paragraph opens the full edition's paragraph editor on it (`ParagraphEditor`, the store and the commit, unforked; the caret nearest the finger, a fingertip's 8 px of slack). The mirror is set in 16 px at least on phones and the header reads "Tap Done to apply" (`setParagraphEditorEdition`); the overlap choice and ⓘ are 44 px there. While a paragraph is open the island steps away and Done rides on the keyboard, as for a text box (EDN-12); Done's press leaves the editor, which commits; the island's Undo takes it back. | EDN-18 | **The phone tool drawer stays open on a tap** (owner, of the tile drawer: "it closes as soon as you pick, and you have to open the tab again to change the colour etc. That's bad. It shouldn't close right away, so I can make another colour choice or undo. Opening and closing isn't hard anyway."). In the tile drawer a tool tile arms its tool with the receive pulse and the drawer stays: the options row turns to the new tool and the island's chip changes under it. A saved signature (both drawers) arms and stays too. The drawer closes only by a press outside, a swipe down, the chip, the head's close button or Esc. Both variants' heads hold the grabber, "Tools" and an icon-only close button (caret down, named "Close tools" / "Araçları kapat", 44 px, quiet, never lime) in place of a text Done, so the island's lime Done, which leaves editing, is the only "Done" on screen (the tiles gain this head; the list's text Done is replaced). New signature still closes it, since its pad is a sheet that needs the screen. **The press outside, per tool:** where the armed tool's next step is a tap on the page (tool-store modes `text-box`, `edit-text`, `signature`: Text, Edit text, a chosen signature) the press closes the drawer and goes on to the page, which acts in the same tap (the box opens with the keyboard, the paragraph opens, the signature is placed); the scrim lets presses through and a capture listener closes (`mark/drawer-close.ts`), and the drawer's close never takes the focus back from what the tap focused. For every other tool (Pen, Highlight, the Eraser, Select, Sign before a choice) the press only closes, on the transparent scrim, so a tap meant to put the drawer away never leaves ink, erases or selects. A press on the island never closes it. **Undo with the drawer open:** the tile drawer floats above the island, whose Undo and Redo stay in reach and leave the drawer open, so its quiet row keeps Eraser, Redo, Select; the list covers the island, so its quiet row gains Undo. Replaces the close after the arming pulse (EDN-16) and Sign's `keepsDrawer` (EDN-14); both drawer variants stay for the owner's comparison. | 2026-10-12 | Owner, conversation (2026-10-12 phone feedback, the tile drawer); implementer (the per-tool rule, technique) | In force |
