# Decisions: Redaction

Redaction behaviour after the spec (`docs/specs/redaction-and-text-editing.md` §1). Ids RED-n belong to this area.

| # | Decision | Date | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| RED-1 | **A mark drawn over a redaction after it was applied does not stop the copy.** The guarantee is that redacted content is gone; an annotation the user creates after the apply (an ink stroke across the black box, a note, a shape) is new content, not redacted content. The export's self-check therefore takes, per applied redaction, the /NM of the annotations created on its source after that apply (`RedactionPlan.laterMarks`, from the engine edits' order in `redactionExportPlan`): `no-annotations-in-areas` does not count them, and `fill-pixels` renders the areas without annotations (check 7 still stops every other annotation in an area). Nothing else is excused: the text, search, object-string and byte checks still run on the whole file, so a later mark that shows a redacted string still fails; a /Redact mark is never excused; the apply's own self-check is unchanged. Before, such a stroke failed checks 7 and 8 and Save a copy and Print refused with "found redacted content", which was false. With Flatten annotations on, the mark becomes page content the pixel check cannot tell from what was under the box, so the copy is still refused; when a later mark lies on an area (`RedactionExportPlan.laterMarksInAreas`, from the /Rect or points of its create edit) the refusal gives the reason and the way out (save without flattening annotations, or move or delete the mark), otherwise the generic refusal stands. | 2026-10-22 | Lead (R25 root-cause lane); `packages/engine/src/redaction/forensic.ts`, `export-hooks.ts`, `later-marks.test.ts`; `e2e/redaction.spec.ts` | In force |
