# Decisions: Text editing

Text-editing robustness work (owner's report of 2026-10-12: real-world files bunch, overlap and refuse retyping; the iPhone space-bar cursor). Ids TXT-n belong to this workstream.

| # | Decision | Date | Source | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TXT-1 | **An emptied paragraph types in the style it had.** Deleting all of a paragraph's text keeps the style of its first removed character (`ParagraphState.typing`, set only while the text is empty), as a word processor does; a paragraph empty from the start types in the input's first style. Before this, typed text took no style: zero width (the glyphs bunched at one x, the caret stood still) and the writer refused the commit. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `apps/web/src/text-edit/`, `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `paragraph-model.ts` | In force |
| TXT-2 | **The layout never measures text with zero width.** `layoutParagraph` treats a style id without metrics as absent and falls back to the caret's style, then the first known style. The writer still refuses empty style ids in the payload. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `apps/web/src/text-edit/`, `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `linebreak.ts` | In force |
| TXT-3 | **Growth room counts what is drawn below, wherever it is drawn.** `gapBelow`/`gapAbove` count graphics inside Form XObjects (up to 8 levels, mapped to the page) and the `/Rect` of visible annotations that take space (form fields, stamps); links, text markup, popups and hidden annotations do not count. A paragraph that grows no longer runs over a logo, a table rule or a field. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `apps/web/src/text-edit/`, `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `paragraph-input.ts` | In force |
| TXT-4 | **Substitute advances are measured as the written font draws them:** whole thousandths of the em, truncated, as `FPDFText_LoadCidType2Font` writes `/W`. Exact advances drifted ~0.007 pt per glyph on a 2048-unit face, so Turkish letters typed into an Inter subset failed the paragraph check. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `apps/web/src/text-edit/`, `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `fonts.ts` | In force |
| TXT-5 | **The paragraph mirror lies on the drawn lines, topmost and hit-testable.** It covers the canvas's box (never `pointer-events: none`), holds one block per drawn line with that line's text scaled onto it, and keeps its font at 16 px or more on phones (no iOS zoom). iOS's space-bar trackpad and point caret placement hit-test the focused editable, so they now land on the text under the drawn line; before, the mirror sat under the canvas and the trackpad did nothing. A press on it places the caret from the drawn lines and wins over the browser's own placement for 500 ms. Hand check: `docs/qa/iphone-text-edit-checklist.md`. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `apps/web/src/text-edit/`, `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `mirror-dom.ts`, `ParagraphEditor.tsx`; EDN-17 | In force; the iPhone hand check is the owner's |
| TXT-6 | **The mirror's DOM positions are read by counting text, and input acts on the browser's target range.** Any (node, offset) in the mirror maps to a text offset from the mirror's start (never inside a surrogate pair); a selection that leaves the focused mirror is put back; `beforeinput` acts on `getTargetRanges()` when given (autocorrect and predictive replacement, grapheme and word deletes), else on the model's selection; the DOM selection is set from the model after every change. Autocorrect no longer doubles words. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `apps/web/src/text-edit/`, `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `mirror-dom.ts`, `ParagraphEditor.tsx` | In force |
| TXT-7 | **Code 32 is the space whenever the text page reads it as one.** In tier-2 code choice, other codes that read as a space (WinAnsi's no-break space 160, often glyphless in a subset) no longer make the space `ambiguous-encoding`. Before, the space got no code, rewritten lines were written without space glyphs, and paragraph detection read words glued ("ofbuilding"), so the next edit opened glued text. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `tier2-codes.ts` | In force |
| TXT-8 | **Rewritten lines write a real space glyph at every word gap the font can draw.** A line stays one object, except: in a style whose spaces advance differently per original object (Tw per line, TJ offsets after spaces) each single space ends its object; a gap of several spaces never ends an object (detection would cut the word after it off as a block); in objects split per glyph (a Tc or Tz a new object cannot carry), a space joins the glyph before it. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `paragraph-edit.ts` | In force |
| TXT-9 | **The writer's verification checks the origins of glyphs with ink only.** Space glyphs must read back but their origin is not held to 0.01 pt (it moves with the advance of the glyph before it in an object without the style's Tc); the glyph after a space starts a new object and is checked. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/` `paragraph-edit.ts` | In force |
| TXT-10 | **A paragraph is justified however the stretch is made.** Its non-final lines share left edges within 0.5 pt and right edges within 0.5 pt, or within ¼ space when some lines have stretched word spaces (median drawn space more than 0.03 × size wider than the narrowest line's), whether Tw, TJ offsets or separate objects stretch them. Word's TJ-justified paragraphs read as `left` before, so an edit reflowed them with wide gaps, grew them and ran them into the next paragraph. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/blocks.ts`, `paragraph-input.ts` | In force |
| TXT-11 | **A line's right edge is its last glyph's advance end,** not PDFium's loose box when that box ends at ink overhanging the advance (an `f`); the advance comes from the run's own glyph steps, else the page's for that font, size and character. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/blocks.ts`, `paragraph-input.ts` | In force |
| TXT-12 | **A straight gutter splits columns even when narrower than twice the page's space:** at least twice the region's natural word space, with the text right of it starting at one x (0.5 pt) on at least 3 lines and ¾ of its lines. A river of stretched spaces is never that straight. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/blocks.ts`, `paragraph-input.ts` | In force |
| TXT-13 | **The rewrap's word gap is the natural gap, never a stretched one,** whatever alignment was detected: the least stretched lines' gap; the space measured in the style's object only when it is not clearly wider. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/blocks.ts`, `paragraph-input.ts` | In force |
| TXT-14 | **A line is split into blocks only at gaps over 3 × the larger of the page's space and the line's own drawn word space,** so two stretched spaces between objects no longer cut the word after them off as a block. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/blocks.ts`, `paragraph-input.ts` | In force |
| TXT-15 | **A rewritten justified line may stretch as far as its paragraph's untouched lines do:** the cap is the larger of 1.5 × the natural word gap (craft §4.3) and the largest stretch seen on the paragraph's original non-final lines, 1.1 × the largest stretch of the paragraph's original justified lines (not the last line, not one ending at a line break), so a rewritten paragraph looks like its untouched lines instead of falling ragged. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/linebreak.ts` | In force (lane 2 building) |
| TXT-16 | **Justified lines write their space glyphs too,** with the justification stretch as the space's offset; the writer starts the next word in a new object, so TXT-8 holds on justified lines (the layout used to close the run at each gap and carry the stretch as the next run's x, so justified lines were written without spaces and read back glued). | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/linebreak.ts` | In force |
| TXT-17 | **A double space reads back as two spaces.** PDFium's text page keeps one glyph of adjacent spaces; a kept space glyph stands for n spaces when its step to the next glyph is within 0.2 of n × the line's smallest space step (n from 2 to 8). Generated spaces (`LocatedGlyph.generated`) never count, and a justified line's stretched spaces never turn into two. Line text, span text, the paragraph input and the app's click mapping all carry the counted spaces. | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/blocks.ts` | In force |
| TXT-18 | **The mirror's clipboard, compositions and clicks do not depend on its DOM or the engine.** Copy and cut write the model's selected text (the mirror's block per line was copied with a line break at every drawn line, which pasted back as hard breaks); a composition replaces the range its first `insertCompositionText` names (Android keyboards compose the word before the caret, which was doubled); an `input` that leaves the mirror's text unlike the model's outside a composition (WebKit commits a composition after `compositionend`) puts the model's lines and selection back; the editor counts double and triple presses itself (`pointerdown` carries no click count in Chromium, so a double click only placed the caret); a key with the input method's key code 229 is the IME's (WebKit sends the Esc that cancels a composition after `compositionend`), never the editor's Esc; inserted text is stored NFC (a pasted g + U+0306 was a mark with no advance, refused as unsupported). | 2026-10-12 | QA lane (text-editing robustness); `apps/web/src/text-edit/ParagraphEditor.tsx`, `e2e/paragraph-mirror.spec.ts` | In force |
| TXT-19 | **A word wider than its line breaks inside itself, as CSS `overflow-wrap: break-word` does.** Only a word that alone cannot fit the line's available width breaks, at the last code point that fits (never inside a surrogate pair or before a combining mark), with no hyphen added; other words wrap whole. The overlay, the caret and the writer see the same lines, Only a single glyph wider than the line still runs over (`overfull`). The line editor's replacement text is NFC too (TXT-18). So a long URL never draws past the measure (before, the editor showed it fitting and the writer refused it, or it ran into the margin or the next column). | 2026-10-12 | Lead (text-editing robustness); `packages/engine/src/text-edit/linebreak.ts`; QA lane finding | In force |
| TXT-20 | **The sample's feature page sends the reader through Edit text, never a double-click.** Fresh-eyes finding on develop f5a2feb: the full cut's try slip on page 3 said "Double-click any paragraph to change its words", but in viewing a double-click selects a word (ADR-0029 §2.3, flows §3.2; `doubleClickOpensEditor` in `viewer/hit-order.ts` opens the editor only in Markup with Select, as built in D1-5 and held by S3 in `input-rules.spec.ts`), so the reader got one selected word and the letters typed next ran as the selection's and the tools' keys (ADR-0029 §2.3, keys on a selection). Whether viewing's double-click should open the editor, and whether letters typed over a double-clicked word should stand down, change ADR-0029 §2.3 and are the owner's; the lead holds them as a proposal. | 2026-10-22 | Lead (R25 root-cause lane, the first five minutes); MOB-11, ADR-0029 §2.3 | In force |
