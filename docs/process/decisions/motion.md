| MOT-12 | **Motion that never stutters (owner, R22 brief C-5): what cost frames, and the rule that follows.** Measured on the production build in Chromium with the CPU slowed 4× and 6× (390 × 844 touch at 3×, 1440 × 900 at 2×; frame deltas, long animation frames, `Performance.getMetrics`, a trace per motion; harness outside the repo). Anything that needs the main thread each frame stutters as soon as it is busy (the menu or the island mounting at the same moment); a software renderer also re-uploads each page canvas on every main-thread frame (5–25 ms each at 4×). A centred dialog grown from its control re-rastered its scaled blurred layer (171 ms of raster a run); the tabs' fill stalled with the document switch it belongs to. **Rule:** a motion moves `transform` and `opacity` on the compositor; nothing is traced, masked, clipped or laid out per frame; and glass that grows out of a control (the container transform's popups, sheets and dialogs out of a control, the phone's menus and morph) is its tier's solid twin with an unblurred rim while it moves (`data-glass-moving`, `ui/glass-moving.ts`, `materials.css`), its filter back under the opaque fill once it rests and its tint and shadow fading in over `--duration-fast` (`data-glass-settle`); a close keeps its glass (short, and nothing grows). **Before → after** (4×, a warm run; presented frames a second while the motion runs; main-thread frames over 50 ms): ⋯ menu open 25 → 46 fps, close 23 → 55, 7 → 1 long frames (6×: 5 → 3); file menu open 15 → 37, close 15 → 52, 6 → 1 (6×: 5 → 1); morph to the island 39 → 53 fps and back 28 → 58, 7–8 → 1 long frames (6×: 8 → 1–2), layouts a run 45 → 6; the dialog out of its control 17 → 26 fps, raster 171 → 81 ms; the tab fill's first switch 26 → 65 fps, longest gap 557 → 29 ms. | MOT-16 | **The phone chrome hides and returns on a spring the scroll starts (owner, R22 brief C-6).** As a scroll hides or brings back the split top's pieces, the capsule and ✎, the scroll's speed (that frame's travel over the time since the last, one frame when the scroll has just begun) picks one of four springs for that move (`data-chrome-spring` on the root, `mark/chrome-spring.ts`), each a `linear()` curve that starts at its step's speed, as a sheet's release does: under 300 px/s a long spring with no bounce (900 ms), then 720 and 640 ms, and from 1 800 px/s a 490 ms spring with bounce 0.2, a 3 % overshoot that coming back reads as the owner's "boing". | MOT-17 | **Keys typed during a view change go where focus is going** (Epic 2, "Every press lands": a field takes the keys typed during its entrance; lead's technique call).
