Recto Glass

The M9 concept for Recto, the design language and the screens built from it, at working size so both can be judged by eye. The page stays solid, controls float as glass in five densities, and a lime light lives beneath the glass. The screens show the interaction model of flows.md: a document opens in viewing, Markup is the one creation state, Lock is a switch, Pages is a zoom away and nothing is lost. The settings apply to everything on this page.

Ambient light

In-house WebGL field at 1/8 resolution, independent of device pixel ratio; colour follows intensity from teal through mint and lime to lemon, dithered by ±0.5 LSB. Still by default; on Auto it drifts here at 15 fps and glides to still after 60 s without input. It pauses when the tab is hidden or the field is off-screen.

Materials

One material in five densities. Bigger surfaces are denser: chips let 21 % of the backdrop through, sheets 7 %. Each tier sits over its worst case, a white page, then over the bare canvas and over the aurora. Dark glass caps the backdrop so a white page is the worst case; light glass floors it so black is.

Screens

The interaction model of flows.md as working mock screens: the Library, a document in viewing, Markup, the title menu with Lock, the Pages grid and saving, then the same model on a phone. Everything is clickable and keyboard reachable; each screen is complete at rest. All documents, names and figures are fictional.

How it works

Six ideas carry the whole model, in plain words.

  • Viewing

    A document opens ready to read, not locked. Click a field and type, select text and act on it, right-click a page for page actions. Nothing changes until you aim at something.

  • Targeted acts

    Select text and a bar offers Copy, Highlight, Comment, Redact and Edit text. Each is one step you can undo, and page text changes only through Edit text.

  • Markup

    Free drawing has one door, Markup, and one exit, Done. The palette opens with Select armed; pick a pen, the highlighter, a shape, a note or a signature. Keys: M P H Esc.

  • Lock

    A switch in the document's title menu. A locked document can still be read, searched, copied and saved as a copy, but not changed, and the dock says Locked. Signed files lock themselves.

  • Pages

    Pages zooms out from the page to every page as a grid. Click to select, then rotate, move, extract or delete; Done zooms back to the page. Key 3.

  • Saving

    Every change is kept on this device within seconds and Undo is always in reach. Save writes into the file you opened and checks it; Save a copy… makes everything else. Nothing is uploaded.

      Decisions. A document opens in viewing: page first, nothing locked, and acts aimed at something work without a mode. The frame is a slim M3 strip (the active title is the document menu), a labelled M2 dock and an M1 page pill. At rest no glass sits over the page: it is shown at Fit page, so the whole page sits in the free rectangle; scroll and the pages pass under the strip and the dock. Markup is the one creation state: the dock morphs into the palette on one glass shape, Done leads it, Select is armed on opening, and the armed tool takes the one lime with the under-light beneath it (a pen takes a ring, since its dot is ink).
