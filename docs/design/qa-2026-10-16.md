## Checked and left as they are

- **Glass (GLS-23) and buttons (GLS-25):** every menu, popover and sheet walked wears M4 or the
  sheet material; no surface on the old recipe and no control outside the shared buttons was seen.
  Light and dark both read; no contrast failure was seen by eye (the proofs are GLS-21, GLS-23).
- **Focus rings:** present on the strip, menus (highlighted row), sheets and the phone's sheets.
  A ring on the phone About's Done in the screenshots comes from the script's Escape (keyboard
  modality), not from a tap.
- **No leftover debug or placeholder text** in either message file: the only "placeholder" strings
  are the form tool's Signature placeholder, a real feature name.
- **Context menus** repeat page commands by rule (DSN-47).
- **Review's chips** show zero counts on an empty document by spec (RA-21: nothing moves under the
  pointer).

## Open: taste questions for the owner

1. **"8 pages" twice in the Pages grid.** The selected tab carries the count (owner feedback F1,
   2026-10-08) and the Pages bar at rest says "8 pages" too, on screen together. Drop the bar's
   count at rest (the bar would read ✓ Done │ ◯ Select │ ⋯) or the tab's? Recommendation: the
   bar's, since the tab's was the owner's own ask.
2. **The pen editor repeats the ink strip.** With a pen armed, the preset editor's Width slider sits
   right above the ink strip's own width slider. Keep both, or hide the strip while the editor is
   open?
3. **OCR languages:** each of nine rows says "Downloads 2.0 MB" and the note below says "the first
   run downloads 2.0 MB". Keep the per-row sizes, or show only the size and drop the verb?
4. **Save a copy has no Cancel** while every other task sheet does (✕ closes it). Intended?
5. **Facts order differs:** desktop "8 pages · 100 KB", phone file menu "PDF · 70.2 KB · 6 pages".
   One order for both?
6. **The bar's Extract is "Yeni belgeye" in Turkish**, short for the context menu's "Yeni belgeye
   taşı". Is the short form clear enough in the bar?
7. **Review's name prompt** says "Name on your comments" where Settings says "Name on comments".
   Same words in both, or keep the prompt's "your"?

## Risk

their single line and height, which the menus spec's 44 px rows check. `:has()` is in every
supported engine (Chromium 105, Safari 15.4, Firefox 121).
